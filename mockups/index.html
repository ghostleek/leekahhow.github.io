how to review

  • Mobile pages look best in a phone-width window (≤ 430 px) or your browser's device toolbar — on a wide screen they render inside a phone frame.
  • Panorama & pivot are swipeable: drag sideways, or hold shift and use the scroll wheel.
  • The theme page persists your choice across all mockups — pick an accent, then revisit the start screen.
  • Everything is flat, lowercase, and chrome-light per the guide; gradients, shadows and rounded corners are deliberately absent. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Open the folder itself with open mockups/index.html in a terminal.
