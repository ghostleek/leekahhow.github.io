  1. No metadata: no meta description, no Open Graph/Twitter cards, no favicon — shared links render as bare URLs and search results say almost nothing.
  2. Nav opens JS hash-“modals”: no real pages, no back/forward history of sections, no focus management for keyboard users; body content is hidden with JS until you click.
  3. 2.8 MB background photo, ~7.2 MB total page — the bg loads even on mobile data; below-fold photos are 1.6 MB and 2.7 MB.
  4. ~2.6 MB of Font Awesome webfonts plus 56 KB of icon CSS ship for ~15 icons; icons are loaded via jQuery-era classes.
  5. jQuery + 4 helper scripts (~113 KB) to do one show/hide animation with nested timeouts.
