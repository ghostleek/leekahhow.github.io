A stack-agnostic sketch of the modernized portfolio: real sections instead of hash-modals, dark-first with an optional light mode, and the four content pillars from your tagline given room to breathe.

Scoping note: gold-numbered markers are optional additions you can drop to keep the change smaller; green markers are the core modernization. Nothing here commits you to a tech stack — it can be built as plain HTML or an Astro/Eleventy site.

  1. Sticky nav with real sections — each link scrolls to a real <section> with its own URL fragment; browser back/forward and sharing just work.
  2. Theme toggle — follows prefers-color-scheme by default, manually overridable, persisted in localStorage.
  3. Hero with one clear goal — big display type and two CTAs instead of four equal links; tagline keeps your four pillars.
  4. Timeline replaces the table — reflows gracefully on mobile and reads better than a borderless table.
  5. Project cards — revive the commented-out Medium card + Gapminder plotly embed as first-class content.
  6. Photography gallery (optional) — 3–4 hero photos with a lightbox; more shots behind a link. Keeps the “Photos” section.
  7. Semantic landmarks + focus management — header/nav/main/footer, skip-link, visible :focus-visible rings, alt text everywhere.
  8. Data + Dance sections (optional) — interactive viz and a bboy video; only if you want to invest in those pages.
  9. Contact that actually contacts — a working form (Formspree/Netlify Forms) or mailto, plus social links; sitemap.xml and robots.txt in the footer.
